Why Little Lightbulbs was used for the draft: a lightbulb is a bright idea, so it keeps the
"little thinkers" meaning; it has no California conflicts and no US child care using the exact
name; it draws well as a crayon logo; and two sensible domains are open. Little Thinking Caps
is closest to the original wording and has its .com available, but a New Jersey preschool
already uses the exact name.

To switch: `./rename.sh "Little Thinking Caps"` (or any other name).

## Official checks to run before committing to a name

These databases block automated access, so they need to be checked by hand:

1. **California business names**: [bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/search/business](https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov/search/business). Search the name. This matters if you form an LLC or corporation. A fictitious business name (DBA) is filed with the county clerk instead; check your county's fictitious business name search too.
2. **Licensed child care facilities in California**: [ccld.dss.ca.gov/carefacilitysearch](https://www.ccld.dss.ca.gov/carefacilitysearch/). Search by facility name. This is the authoritative list of licensed programs.
3. **Federal trademarks**: [tmsearch.uspto.gov](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/). Search the name and look at Class 41 (education and child care services) and Class 43 (day care centers).

Also worth grabbing early: the Instagram and Facebook handles for whichever name wins.
